# Exam Guide

Everything you need to understand the **Symfony 8 Certification** before you start
studying: what the exam is, how it is scored, how the two levels differ, and how to
study and sit it efficiently.

!!! abstract "The exam at a glance"
    - **75 questions** in **90 minutes** (~72 seconds each).
    - **Single choice, multiple choice, and true/false** questions.
    - One exam, two outcomes: **Advanced** or **Expert**, decided by your score.
    - **PHP 8.4+** baseline (a Symfony 8 requirement).
    - Covers the **15 official topic areas**; Messenger is up-weighted, HTTP Caching
      down-weighted versus Symfony 7.
